The game

Grand strategy you can play in a coffee break

Deep enough to master, clear enough to learn in minutes. Every turn brings gold, food, horses and manpower, and every decision counts.

A living map of Eurasia

Command your armies across a hand-painted hex map from the Rus forests to the Song cities. Rival rulers expand, trade and go to war on their own, so no two campaigns play the same.

War is more than numbers

Battles are decided by terrain, fortresses and morale. Besiege capitals, guard the passes and strike where the enemy is weak.

85 tactical cards

From legendary stallions to siege engines, the right card at the right moment can turn a losing war.

Diplomacy that matters

Sign trade agreements, non-aggression pacts and alliances, accept a truce or declare war. Hold the Silk Road and your treasury overflows.

Build your empire

Camps, markets, workshops, stables, bridges, fortresses and wonders. Grow each province into the backbone of your realm.

The Moose, your guide on the steppe

Ask the Moose about the rules at any time. A complete rulebook and a world chronicle of the 13th century are always one tap away.

Play your way, anywhere

No account and no connection needed for single player. Autosave, five save slots, easy, normal and hard difficulty, and fourteen languages.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Arojen Tarinat free to play?

Yes. The game is free to download and play, with adverts. Arojen Premium is an optional monthly subscription that removes every advert; it unlocks no gameplay advantage.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Campaigns against the AI work without an internet connection and save automatically. Online multiplayer, chat and the cloud copy of your progress need a connection.

How does online multiplayer work?

Two to four players each lead a realm and the AI plays the others. Create a private room and share its code or invite link, open a public room, or use quick match to join an open game. Turns are played in order, and if a player leaves, the AI takes over their realm.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play everything as a guest. Signing in with Google or email is optional and keeps a copy of your progress and achievements in the cloud, so you can continue on a new phone.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or newer, from Google Play. There is also a version you can play in a web browser.

Which languages does the game speak?

Fourteen: English, Finnish, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, Bengali and Indonesian.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the game, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. You can also email us. The account deletion page explains what is removed.
