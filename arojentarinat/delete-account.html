Delete your account

You can delete your Arojen Tarinat account and the data linked to it at any time, right in the game or by email.

In the game

Three steps, done at once

  1. Open Settings

    Start Arojen Tarinat and open Settings from the main menu.

  2. Go to Account

    Scroll to the Account section, where you see the email or Google account you signed in with.

  3. Delete account

    Tap Delete account and confirm. Your account and its data are deleted straight away, and the game continues as a guest on this phone.

Without the app

Ask us by email

Email us from the address you signed in with, or tell us which Google account you used. We confirm it is your account and delete it within 30 days.

Email contact@arojentarinat.com

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address or Google sign-in
  • The cloud copy of your progress, saves and achievements
  • Your chat and voice messages in every match
  • Your multiplayer name, which is replaced with “Player”; matches in progress continue with the AI leading your realm

What stays, and for how long

  • Progress saved on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Pseudonymous gameplay statistics are not linked to your account and are kept for at most 14 months.
  • The audio files of voice messages can stay in our private storage, no longer shown to anyone, until they are cleaned up. Email us to have them removed at once.
  • Arojen Premium records are tied to the Google Play purchase, not to your account. Google keeps its own records of purchases made through Google Play.

Subscribed to Arojen Premium? Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

More details are in our privacy policy.
