About Arojen Tarinat

An independent strategy game from Finland about Genghis Khan, the Mongol Empire and the realms that stood in its way. Made by two developers who love history, maps and fair game design.

Our story

Tales of the Steppe

Arojen tarinat is Finnish for tales of the steppes. The game starts in 1206, the year Temüjin united the tribes of the steppe as Genghis Khan, and lets you decide what happens next.

We wanted a strategy game with the depth of the classic grand strategy titles that still fits on a phone and in a coffee break: clear rules, short turns and decisions that matter. Every realm has its own strengths, every province its own value, and every campaign its own story.

Arojen Tarinat began as a game you can play in a web browser, came to Google Play, and has grown with every version since. Today it is a fully native Android game with online multiplayer, played in fourteen languages.

The journey

How the tale grew

  1. The beginning

    A strategy game for the browser

    The first Arojen Tarinat ran in any web browser: the map, the realms, the cards and the five paths to victory.

  2. Google Play

    The steppe in your pocket

    The game arrived on Android phones and tablets, free to play on Google Play.

  3. Version 2.0

    Rebuilt as a native Android game

    Faster, smoother and redesigned from the ground up, with fourteen languages, achievements and ranks, a complete rulebook, a world chronicle and the Moose as your guide.

  4. Version 2.1

    Online together

    Multiplayer for two to four players with chat and voice messages, a daily challenge with player levels, accounts with a cloud copy of your progress, and Arojen Premium.

What we stand for

Our principles

Fair to every player

Everything that wins a campaign is earned by playing. Arojen Premium only removes adverts; it never sells power.

Rooted in history

Real realms, real rulers and a world chronicle of the 13th century, told with respect for the people who lived it.

Respect for your data

No account needed, analytics only with your consent, and your data deleted when you ask. Read our privacy policy.

Made for everyone

Fourteen languages, offline play and modest phone requirements, so the steppe is open to players everywhere.
