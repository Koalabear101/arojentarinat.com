Delete your account

You can delete your Arojen Tarinat account and the data linked to it at any time, right in the game or by email.

Last updated 1 November 2026

On this page

  1. Delete in the game
  2. Delete by email
  3. What is deleted
  4. What stays
  5. Arojen Premium
  6. Playing without an account

Delete in the game

The quickest way. Three taps, and it happens at once.

  1. Open Settings

    Start Arojen Tarinat and open Settings from the main menu.

  2. Go to Account

    Scroll to the Account section, where you see the email or Google account you signed in with.

  3. Tap Delete account

    Confirm, and your account and its data are deleted straight away. The game carries on as a guest on this phone.

Delete by email

No longer have the game installed? Email us from the address you signed in with, or tell us which Google account you used. We confirm the account is yours and delete it within 30 days. Our address is contact@arojentarinat.com.

Email us

What is deleted

  • Your account: the email address or Google sign-in
  • The cloud copy of your progress, saves and achievements
  • Your chat and voice messages in every match
  • Your multiplayer name, which becomes “Player”. Matches in progress go on with the AI leading your realm.

What stays, and for how long

  • Progress saved on your phone, until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Pseudonymous gameplay statistics, not linked to your account, for at most 14 months.
  • Audio files of voice messages can stay in our private storage, shown to no one, until they are cleaned up. Email us to have them removed at once.

Arojen Premium

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Premium belongs to the Google Play purchase, not to your account, and Google keeps its own records of purchases.

Playing without an account

You never need an account to play. Without one, our servers keep only what online matches need, and uninstalling the game removes everything from your phone. Read more in our privacy policy.
